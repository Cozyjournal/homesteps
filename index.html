Is HomeSteps really free?

Yes! The daily tracker, all 7 worksheet types, and full syllabus are 100% free forever.

Where is my data stored?

All data stays private on your local phone or computer browser. Nothing is sent to external servers.

🔒 Privacy & Child Safety Policy

1. Data Minimization: HomeSteps only stores your child's first name/nickname and daily learning records. Photos, addresses, and private personal details are never collected.

2. 100% Local-First: All data remains on your local device. There are zero third-party tracking scripts or advertising SDKs.

3. Export & Delete Anytime: You can download a complete JSON backup of your records at any time or reset data with one click.
